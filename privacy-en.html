Privacy policy

Last updated: 27.10.2026

1. Data controller

The controller of the personal data of BubuFriend users is Konrad Kozubek (brand 0707), e-mail: kontakt@0707.pl. You can contact me at this address about anything related to your personal data.

2. What data we process

• Account data: e-mail address, Sign in with Apple identifier (if you use it). • Profile data: name, nickname, gender, date of birth, chosen theme, language and time zone. • Couple content: messages, photos, videos (reels), feelings, hearts, requests, shopping lists, plans, wishlist, couple finances, answers to questions and the quiz, trips. • Health data (optional): menstrual cycle and symptom entries. • 18+ zone data (optional): the to-try list and private votes. • Location (optional): only while you share it for a chosen time or during a trip. During a trip you can turn on sharing with the screen off — a notification is shown the whole time, and sharing ends with the trip or when you close the app. We never collect location without your knowledge. • Technical data: push notification token, basic server logs and app error reports (error text, screen and app version — without the content of your messages or entries).

3. Purposes and legal bases

• Providing the service and running your account — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract). • Health data (cycle) and 18+ zone data — Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR, based on your explicit consent given by turning these features on. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature off and deleting the entries. • Security and error handling — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). We do not sell data, do not show ads and do not profile users for marketing.

4. Who can see your data

Couple content is visible only to you and the person you connected with. Access is restricted at the database level. Some exceptions are intentional: your partner sees answers to the question of the day and the quiz only after giving their own, the wishlist owner does not see gift reservations, and in the 18+ zone your partner only sees shared matches. The person whose cycle data it is decides how much is shared (everything / phase only / nothing).

5. Processors

• Supabase Inc. — database, sign-in and file storage; servers in the European Union (Ireland). • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) — push notification delivery; may transfer data to the USA under standard contractual clauses. • Apple Inc. — Sign in with Apple and notifications (APNs). • Google LLC — notifications on Android (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and maps on Android (Google Maps). • OSRM / OpenStreetMap — route calculation for trips (only the start and destination coordinates are sent). • Google — payment for BubuFriend Premium (Google Play). We only store the purchase ID to turn Premium on for your couple; we never see card details. All providers process data only on our behalf.

6. How long we keep data

We keep data until the account is deleted, and error reports for 30 days. Disappearing reels stop being visible after 24 hours, shared locations after the chosen time. After a couple disconnects, shared content is archived and no longer visible in the app. After account deletion, data is removed within 30 days, except backups, which expire within another 30 days.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself in the app (More → Delete account). You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).

8. Age

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. The 18+ zone is available only when both partners are 18 or older and have both turned it on.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (TLS), files are kept in private storage accessed through temporary signed links, and database security rules control access to couple data. You can also turn on the Face ID or fingerprint lock.

10. Changes to this policy

We will inform you in the app about material changes. The current version is always available under More → Privacy policy.
